# Working on this OwnEngine project (for AI agents)

Kongal (콩알단) walking around a small meadow: a toon-shaded, outlined skinned character moved with W/A/S/D or
arrows (left stick), Shift to run, Q/E to orbit the camera.

- `scenes/main.scene.json` is generated by `node tools/make_scene.cjs scenes/main.scene.json` (meadow, props,
  player, camera post settings); change the generator and run it again rather than editing the scene by hand.
- Character: `Player` (CharacterBody sphere r=0.34 centred at body height, `scripts/kongal_player.lua`) >
  `Kongal` (model, `Animator` Idle/Walk, faces +Z). The script turns `Kongal` toward the walk direction,
  scales the Walk cadence with speed and moves the camera's `CameraFollow.offset` for Q/E.
- Model: `assets/models/kongal_v7_anim.glb` from the Kongal Blender rig (10 bones, Idle 2.04 s, Walk 1.04 s).
  The feet are skinned into the belly, so stepping deforms the body instead of moving rigid feet.
- Color space: `project.json` has `"rendering": {"colorSpace": "linear"}` (docs/RENDERING.md "Color space"), like the
  viewer (three.js, sRGB output): scene and material colors are written as sRGB and decoded by the engine; shader
  graph constants and uniforms are linear numbers (`meadow.shader.json` `colorA`/`colorB` and `blob.shader.json`
  `tint` hold the decoded values of the old gamma colors).
- Look (`shaders/`, `materials/`):
  - `toon.shader.json` on lit materials (generated: `node tools/make_toon.cjs shaders/toon.shader.json`, 127
    nodes) is the viewer's gouache material (kongal/viewer/src/gouacheMaterial.js: `paintLight`, `brushDabs`, the
    boundary wobble and pigment grain) as an engine light graph (docs/POSTPROCESS.md "Light stage") with the viewer's
    own colors decoded, not values fitted by eye: the hemisphere ambient is the engine `ambient` input (the Sun's
    `ambient`/`groundAmbient` = sky #f6ead4 / ground #d0a070 x 0.82), the sun band
    `smoothstep(-0.32, 0.32, N.L + wobble) * lightAttenuation` takes the Sun's color (#ffe6b8 x 0.45), and `termK`,
    `dabShadeK`/`dabLightK` and `rimColor`/`rimDir` are the terminator, brush dabs and backlight rim. Like the
    viewer the noise is laid over the rest pose (`objectPosition`, so it stays on the surface while the rig
    animates) and the rim reads `viewDirection`. With one sun it is the viewer's formula exactly; extra point/spot
    lights add their own band (their ambient part is counted once per light). It starts from `baseColor`, which
    already includes the base texture (do not multiply `texture`).
  - Camera `PostProcess`: warm grading, bloom, a warm vignette (`vignetteColor` brown, as the viewer), cream fog and the post graph
    `shaders/watercolor.shader.json` (`"stage": "post"`, 64 nodes): the three.js WatercolorPass with its values
    folded into uniforms (`wobble` = 1.4 x 2 px, `bleed`, `edgeDarken` x 0.5, `wash` and `granulation` x 2,
    `paper` x 2). It is generated by `node tools/make_watercolor.cjs shaders/watercolor.shader.json` (named
    nodes); tune it through `PostProcess.shaderUniforms`.
  - Outlines are the engine material fields `outlineWidth` + `outlineUnits: "pixels"` (2.2 px like the three.js
    prototype; flowers 1.5 px) and `outlineColor` (docs/RENDERING.md "Outlines"): the same screen thickness at
    any camera distance, no outline entities. The shells follow smoothed normals, so cube trunks keep a closed line at their corners.
  - `meadow.shader.json`: lit ground with two layers of sine patches between `colorA` and `colorB`.
- Matching the three.js prototype (`E:\Projects\Game\kongal\viewer`): `node tools/make_compare.cjs
  scenes/compare.scene.json 0.5` builds a scene framed like the viewer's default view (camera, fov 30, light
  direction, Idle held at 0.5 s, cream backdrop, `materials/blob.mat.json` contact blob); render it with
  `oe render scenes/compare.scene.json --renderer gpu --width 960 --height 720` and capture the viewer with
  `?gui=off&pose=0.5&model=kongal_v7_anim.glb` at 960x720, device scale 1. With the full port the body is within
  a mean 3.6 / 3.9 / 2.1 (R/G/B) of the viewer (uv dabs and the fresnel built from cameraPosition: 4.1 / 3.8 / 2.6; the
  gamma version fitted by eye: 5.4 / 7.9 / 3.6).
- The main scene's Skybox (intensity 0.9) gives about the same ambient as the hemisphere of the compare scene; its
  shadows are soft (the viewer's sun is weaker than its ambient), `shadowStrength` 0.6 keeps the trees' visible.
- Verify: `oe render . --renderer gpu --frames 60 --out shot.png`; play-test by piping `input.key {key, down}`,
  `sim.step {frames}` and `script.eval` (read `scene.get(...)`) into `oe script .`.
- Font: `assets/fonts/NanumMyeongjo-Bold.ttf` (SIL OFL, licence beside it) for Hangul UI text.
